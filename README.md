# Miswak-Case
*Es Selamun Aleykum ve Rahmetullahi ve Berakatuh*
Bismillah.

Miswak is one of the most effective, simple, and practical tools used for dental cleaning in various geographies since ancient civilizations, and its use is Sunnah.
However, preserving a miswak is not always that easy.
Especially for fresh (moist) miswaks, keeping them clean, preventing mold, and carrying them around without losing their natural juice can sometimes be quite difficult.
That is why we designed the miswak case.
The miswak case is designed so that you can safely carry either a fresh or dry miswak anywhere you wish.

- It can be carried easily thanks to its simple structure and belt clip.
- Its body structure can store miswaks of various sizes.
- Its air holes prevent molding.
- With its protective structure, it keeps your miswak clean and safe while preserving its natural juice for as long as possible.

Moreover, it is completely free.
You can produce the miswak case yourself using a simple 3D printer.
> **Printing recommendation:** When printing the model, it is recommended to print with a brim to increase bed adhesion.
